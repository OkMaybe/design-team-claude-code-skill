# Conversion Tactics Reference
90 tactics organized by funnel stage. Each: name, action, psychology principle.

## 1. First Impressions (Homepage/Landing)

**Hero Clarity**: Single value proposition above fold, one primary CTA. Pre-attentive processing.

**CTA Visual Cues**: Arrows, whitespace directing eye toward CTA. Visual salience guides attention.

**One Page One Goal**: Each page serves one conversion goal. Fewer distractions = clearer decisions.

**Authentic Imagery**: Real photos over stock. Trust through perceived honesty.

**Benefit-Driven Headlines**: Lead with user gains, not product features. Self-referential encoding.

**Visual Hierarchy**: Size for headlines, contrast for CTAs, whitespace for breathing. Reduces cognitive strain.

**Ad-to-Page Consistency**: Mirror ad colors, copy, visuals on landing page. Pattern recognition builds trust.

**F/Z-Pattern Layouts**: F for text-heavy, Z for simple pages. Aligns with natural scanning.

**Contrast Color CTAs**: CTA color should not appear elsewhere. Visual salience.

**Minimal Navigation**: Remove nav links on landing pages. Fewer escape routes = more conversions.

## 2. Forms and Checkout

**Single-Column Forms**: Vertical flow reduces errors by 15-20%.

**Progress Indicators**: Step count or progress bar. Goal Gradient Effect.

**Auto-Fill and Smart Defaults**: Pre-populate with browser data. +30% completion.

**Inline Validation**: Flag errors in real-time. +22% completion.

**Trust Icons**: SSL badges, payment logos near payment fields.

**Essential Fields Only**: Remove non-essential fields. Hick's Law.

**Guest Checkout**: Offer without account creation. Removes commitment barrier.

**Cart Persistence**: Save cart data for returning users. 8-10% recovery.

**Multiple Payment Methods**: Cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, local methods. -20% friction.

**Simple Coupon UX**: Auto-apply, validate instantly, show discount in total.

## 3. CTA Boosters

**Button-Shaped CTAs**: Buttons signal clickability. Visual affordance.

**Action-Oriented Text**: Get My Free Trial instead of Submit. Reward-driven language.

**Urgency Cues**: Time or scarcity-based, must be authentic. Loss aversion.

**CTA Consistency**: Same text at hero, mid-page, footer. Familiarity.

**Hover Effects**: Subtle color/shadow change. Micro-interactions.

**Microcopy Reassurance**: No credit card required under CTA. Decision-point friction reduction.

**Sticky Mobile CTAs**: Floating bar at bottom. Always within reach.

**Personalized CTA Text**: Tailor to user segment. Self-referential encoding.

**First-Person Phrasing**: Start My Free Trial vs Your. Ownership language +25-90%.

**Placement Testing**: Test below testimonials, after benefits, in hero.

## 4. Trust and Social Proof

**Reviews Near CTAs**: Place reviews adjacent to conversion buttons. Social proof at decision point.
**Star Ratings with Numbers**: 4.8/5 (2847 reviews) beats stars alone. Specificity.
**Client/Partner Logos**: Recognizable brand logos. Authority principle.
**Customer Count**: Trusted by 50,000+ teams. Bandwagon effect.
**Real Testimonials**: Full names, photos, roles, specific outcomes.
**User-Generated Content**: Real customers using product. Authenticity.
**Media Mentions**: As Seen In logos. Third-party authority.
**Live Notifications**: Real-time purchase notifications. Social proof + FOMO.
**Guarantees and Refund Policies**: Display risk-reversal prominently.
**Before/After Comparisons**: Show transformation. Contrast effect.

## 5. Persuasive Copy

**Benefits Over Features**: Save 10 hours/week vs Advanced AI. Outcome focus.
**Objection Handling**: Cancel anytime, No hidden fees. Preemptive anxiety removal.
**Numbers and Statistics**: 37% faster, 10,000+ users. Concrete data anchors value.
**Scannable Chunks**: Short paragraphs, bullets, subheadings. Users read 20% of text.
**Scarcity Cues**: Limited stock, exclusive access. Must be authentic.
**Personalized Messages**: Name, location, behavior data. Relevance.
**Power Words**: Exclusive, proven, effortless, instant. Emotional triggers.
**Plain Language**: Replace jargon. Cognitive fluency = trust.
**FAQs Near Conversion**: Address last-minute doubts before CTA.
**Headline Testing**: Test benefit, curiosity, social proof angles.

## 6. Visual Design and Psychology

**Generous Whitespace**: Reduces overload, increases comprehension.
**Color Psychology**: Blue=trust, green=growth, orange=urgency.
**Readable Font Sizes**: Minimum 16px body. Readability = usability.
**Typography Hierarchy**: Display > heading > body > caption. Speeds scanning.
**Contrast for Emphasis**: Important elements visually distinct. Von Restorff Effect.
**Human Faces**: Connect with faces near CTAs. Hardwired detection.
**Subtle Motion**: 150-300ms micro-animations. Guides attention.
**Trust Icons**: Security locks, checkmarks, verified badges.
**Accessibility as Conversion**: Accessible = more users = good business.
**Friendly Loading**: Skeleton screens, progress indicators.

## 7. Mobile UX

**Mobile-First**: Phone first, scale up. Constraints force clarity.
**Thumb-Zone Placement**: Primary actions in easy-reach zones. Fitts's Law.
**Large Touch Targets**: 44x44px minimum, 8px gaps.
**Progressive Disclosure**: Essentials first, details on demand.
**Reduced Density**: Less text, tighter hierarchy. Shorter attention.
**Fast Page Load**: Under 3 seconds. +32% bounce per second.
**Bottom Navigation**: Key actions within thumb reach.
**Gesture Support**: Swipe, pull to refresh. Natural interactions.
**One-Handed Operability**: Critical flows with one thumb.
**Offline State Handling**: Graceful degradation with messaging.

## 8. Retention

**Thank-You Page Upsells**: Related items on confirmation page.
**Social Sharing**: Easy sharing with pre-written messages.
**Confirmation Emails**: Clear next steps. Reduces anxiety.
**Onboarding Tours**: Tooltips, checklists, progress bars.
**Personalized Follow-Ups**: Behavior-triggered emails.
**Referral Incentives**: Give and Get mechanics. Reciprocity.
**Gamification**: Progress trackers, badges, streaks.
**Clean Dashboards**: Organized, scannable, action-oriented.
**Order Status Tracking**: Real-time progress bars.
**Easy Support Access**: Visible help without intrusion.

## 9. Advanced Testing

**A/B One Variable**: Isolating variables = actionable data.
**Heatmap Tracking**: Behavior data beats assumptions.
**Exit-Intent Popups**: Sparingly with genuine value.
**Video Demos**: Visual demonstration reduces uncertainty.
**Chatbot/Live Chat**: Instant answers at decision point.
**Dynamic Personalization**: Content adapts to behavior/segment.
**Above-Fold Speed**: Perceived > actual speed.
**Authentic Scarcity Timers**: Real deadlines only. Fake urgency kills trust.
**Audience-Segmented CTAs**: Targeted framing per user type.
**Continuous Iteration**: Compound improvements beat big redesigns.
